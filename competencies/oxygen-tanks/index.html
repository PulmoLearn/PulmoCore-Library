Build a Safe Cylinder Routine

The competency is a sequence. Secure the cylinder first, inspect and prepare the outlet, connect compatible equipment, check for leaks, confirm enough gas is available, then deliver and shut down safely.

1

IDENTIFY

Correct Gas + Correct Regulator

Verify cylinder contents by its label and select a compatible regulator or reducing valve before moving forward.

2

MOVE

Secure Before Transport

Release the storage restraint only when ready to move the cylinder. Place it on a portable carrier or cart, secure it, transport it, and secure it again if removed from the cart.

3

CONNECT

Prepare the Outlet and Regulator

Remove cap/wrapInspect outletPoint away + warnCrack cylinderAttach compatible regulator
Safety anchor: Make sure the sealing washer is in place and the regulator/flow control is off before tightening and opening the cylinder.
4

VERIFY

Open, Check, Calculate

Open the valve until the pressure gauge stops rising, turn the valve stem back one-half turn, listen for leaks, then calculate duration of flow from the pressure gauge and the correct tank conversion factor.

5

FINISH

Shut Down Completely

Close the valve, bleed pressure from the regulator, turn the flowmeter off, remove the regulator, replace the safety cap, label and return the cylinder appropriately, discard supplies, remove PPE, perform hand hygiene, and document.

Practice note: Each scenario supplies the cylinder size, pressure, ordered flow, and planned use time. Use the tank-factor chart in the Check & Calculate section to choose the correct factor before calculating duration.

Safe Cylinder Routine Established

Competency Routine

  • Verify cylinder contents by label and color and select a compatible regulator
  • Transport and secure the cylinder safely
  • Inspect, crack, connect, and tighten the regulator correctly
  • Open, check for leaks, calculate duration, and identify unexpected outcomes
  • Set the prescribed flow
  • Close, bleed, remove, cap, choose “empty” or “in use,” return, and document
