Hand Hygiene & Isolation Precautions

Practice recognizing isolation precautions, selecting the correct room sign, gathering supplies, applying PPE, performing hand hygiene, and removing PPE safely.

1 of 7

Orientation block

Competency Overview

Preparation

Review the patient chart for the isolation precaution and gather the necessary hand-hygiene supplies and PPE.

Implementation

Prepare the hands and wrists, then perform either alcohol-based or soap-and-water hand hygiene correctly.

Isolation & Completion

Select the appropriate protective wear, remove PPE to reduce cross-contamination, dispose of it appropriately, and perform final hand hygiene.

Practice goal: connect patient information to the correct isolation sign and PPE before rehearsing the procedural portions of the competency.
