What You Need to Know Before You Practice

1

Standard Precautions Apply to Every Patient

Hand hygiene is always part of Standard Precautions. Gloves are task-based, not automatically required for every patient touch. Use gloves when the planned task may involve blood, body fluids, mucous membranes, non-intact skin, or contaminated equipment.

Example: Taking a pulse on intact skin does not automatically require gloves. Suctioning secretions does.
2

Choose One Hand-Hygiene Method

For routine care when hands are not visibly soiled, use alcohol-based hand rub. Use soap and water when hands are visibly soiled or another specific indication applies.

Not visibly soiled → Alcohol-based hand rub Visible soil / special indication → Soap & water
3

Gloves Do Not Replace Hand Hygiene

Clean hands→ Gloves if indicated→ Patient care→ Remove gloves→ Clean hands
