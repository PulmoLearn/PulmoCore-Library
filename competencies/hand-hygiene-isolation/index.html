Orientation block

Competency Overview

Preparation

Review the chart for the isolation precaution and gather hand-hygiene supplies and appropriate PPE.

Implementation

Prepare the hands and wrists, then complete alcohol-based or soap-and-water hand hygiene.

Isolation & Completion

Select protective wear, remove PPE to prevent cross-contamination, dispose of PPE, and perform hand hygiene.

Prototype goal: show how one hands-on competency can become a sequence of reusable digital practice activities before lab.
