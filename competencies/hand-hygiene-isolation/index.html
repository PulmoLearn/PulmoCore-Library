Hand Hygiene & Isolation Precautions

Work through four patient cases. For each patient, identify the correct isolation sign, gather the needed supplies, and prepare the healthcare worker before moving to the next patient.

Overview

Competency practice

How This Practice Works

Four Patient Cases

You will work through an airborne, droplet, contact, and standard-precautions case. You must complete each patient before the next patient appears.

Clinical Flow

For each patient: interpret the case → choose the room sign → gather supplies → prepare the healthcare worker.

Procedure Practice

After all four patient cases, arrange the hand-hygiene and PPE-removal steps into the correct order using drag and drop.

Goal: use patient information to make the same decisions that will be required before entering a room in the clinical environment.
