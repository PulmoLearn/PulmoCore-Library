Educational Disclaimer

PulmoLearn is for education, not patient-specific medical direction.

PulmoLearn provides respiratory therapy learning materials, clinical calculators, ABG practice, and board-preparation resources to support education and professional development.

Scope of Use

Educational purposes only

PulmoLearn content is provided for educational purposes only and is intended to support respiratory therapy learning, review, and professional development. PulmoLearn does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or patient-specific clinical direction.

Not a medical device or clinical decision-support system

PulmoLearn calculators, lessons, examples, and practice cases are educational tools. They are not intended to function as a medical device, diagnostic tool, clinical decision-support system, or substitute for independent professional judgment.

Use clinical judgment and verified resources

Healthcare decisions should be based on provider orders, institutional policies, manufacturer instructions for use, current clinical standards, verified references, and the judgment of appropriately licensed professionals.

Calculator results must be independently verified

Any calculator output should be checked for accuracy and clinical appropriateness before use in any educational, simulation, or clinical discussion. PulmoLearn is not responsible for errors caused by incorrect inputs, outdated information, or misuse of calculator results.

No guarantee of exam performance

PulmoLearn is designed to support learning and board preparation, but use of the platform does not guarantee success on any examination, certification, course, or clinical evaluation.

For patient care: Always follow your facility policies, provider orders, scope of practice, manufacturer guidance, and applicable laws or regulations.
