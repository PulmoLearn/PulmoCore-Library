INSTRUCTIONAL FLOW
1. Teach question types and why each is used.
2. Guided interview with a patient who has little respiratory history.
3. Independent branching interview using John Jones and the incomplete respiratory evaluation note.
4. Final competency review.

CORE INTERACTION RULE
A follow-up question may appear on screen before it is clinically appropriate. If the learner clicks it before the prerequisite question has established relevance, the activity responds with “Ask another question first” and explains which kind of information must be established.

CONTENT BOUNDARY
The activity is intentionally limited to AHRC 254 Week 1.1: record review, focused respiratory interview, baseline comparison, smoking/vaping/exposure history, home respiratory therapy, first-look assessment, basic current respiratory data, subjective/objective information, data completeness, and verification. It does not ask the learner to diagnose disease or select advanced treatment.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload the folder contents together and launch index.html. The activity uses no external libraries and is mobile responsive.
