Expected compensation: is the other system doing enough?

Competency focus: In metabolic acidosis, use Winter's formula to decide whether respiratory compensation is appropriate or whether a second primary respiratory disorder is present.
Expected PaCO₂ ≈ 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8, ± 2 mmHg

A measured PaCO₂ within the expected range supports appropriate respiratory compensation. A value above the range suggests added respiratory acidosis. A value below the range suggests added respiratory alkalosis.

Competency focus: Distinguish saturation, hemoglobin species, ventilation trends, noninvasive CO₂ trends, and direct blood-gas measurement.

Continuous estimate of oxygen saturation.

Co-oximetry

Continuous exhaled CO₂ and waveform.

Transcutaneous

Noninvasive trend with lag/skin/perfusion limits.

Baseline matters

Chronic hypercapnia and physiologic pregnancy can have expected baseline differences. Ask whether the current values fit the patient's documented baseline and whether the trend suggests a new acute problem.
A “normal” population range is not the only comparison that matters.
