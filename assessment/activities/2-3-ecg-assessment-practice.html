Different ECG systems answer different questions

Competency focus: Select bedside monitoring, telemetry, or a diagnostic 12-lead according to symptoms, mobility, setting, and diagnostic need.

Bedside monitor

Continuous rhythm/rate and alarm surveillance for a patient in a fixed high-acuity location.

Telemetry

Continuous wireless rhythm surveillance for a stable patient who needs to ambulate.

Diagnostic 12-lead

A diagnostic snapshot from multiple electrical perspectives when ischemia, injury, conduction, or a new concerning rhythm must be evaluated.

Important: Sinus rhythm on telemetry does not rule out myocardial ischemia. Continuous rhythm surveillance and a diagnostic 12-lead are complementary, not interchangeable.
