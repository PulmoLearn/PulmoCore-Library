Why the predicted-value inputs matter

Age

Expected lung-function values change across the lifespan.

Standing height

Taller people generally have larger expected lung volumes. A wrong height can substantially distort percent predicted.

Sex / reference-equation input

The selected reference equation uses this demographic input when generating expected values.

Do not copy an unverified height. If the patient's actual height is 180 cm but 150 cm is entered, the raw maneuver may still be usable, but the predicted comparison must be regenerated after correcting the demographic entry.

What breath is an FVC measuring?

Forced vital capacity begins after the patient inhales as deeply as possible and then exhales as much air as possible with a hard, fast start and a sustained finish.

Suggested coaching language

“Take the biggest breath you possibly can. Seal your lips tightly around the mouthpiece. Now BLAST the air out hard and fast—keep going, keep going, keep going...”

The exact script can sound natural. What matters is that the patient understands the maximal inhalation, immediate forceful start, tight seal, and continued exhalation.

FVC Spirometry Study Review

Predicted-value inputs

  • Age
  • Accurate standing height
  • Sex/reference-equation input required by the selected equation and facility policy

FVC breath

Maximal inspiration followed by an immediate, hard, fast, sustained exhalation. FVC includes inspiratory reserve volume + tidal volume + expiratory reserve volume. Residual volume remains.

Quality first

  • Full inspiration before starting
  • Tight mouthpiece seal
  • No hesitation before the blast
  • Hard, fast start
  • Continue exhaling until complete
  • Recognize cough, leak, early termination, glottic closure, and variable effort
Coaching strategy: correct one specific error at a time, demonstrate again when useful, give clear encouragement, and allow rest when needed.
Safety: stop and assess if the patient develops concerning symptoms such as new chest pressure or presyncope. Do not push through instability just to complete the test.
Key rule: Quality first, interpretation second.
