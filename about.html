About PulmoLearn

Respiratory therapy education built by an RT educator.

PulmoLearn was created to help respiratory therapy students connect classroom content, bedside reasoning, and board-style thinking through interactive lessons, clinical calculators, ABG practice, and high-yield review tools.

Founder & Educator

Created by an RRT for RRTs

PulmoLearn was created by Melissa Wells, an experienced respiratory therapist, educator, instructional designer, and respiratory therapy program director. The platform was built from the perspective of someone who has cared for critically ill patients at the bedside, taught future respiratory therapists in the classroom and lab, and designed curriculum to help students move beyond memorization toward true clinical reasoning.

The goal of PulmoLearn is simple: make respiratory therapy content clearer, more interactive, and more clinically connected so students can build confidence before clinicals, prepare for exams, and enter the profession with stronger reasoning skills.

Why PulmoLearn Exists

Built for students who need more than a textbook.

Respiratory therapy students are asked to learn complex physiology, equipment, disease processes, patient assessment, ABG interpretation, and critical care decision-making in a short amount of time. PulmoLearn organizes that learning into interactive resources that are easier to revisit, practice, and apply.

Clinical Reasoning First

Lessons are designed to help students understand why findings matter and how RTs use data to make patient-care decisions.

Board-Aligned Thinking

Content supports the pattern recognition, prioritization, and interpretation students need for NBRC-style preparation.

Interactive by Design

PulmoLearn combines lessons, calculators, ABG practice, quick references, and review tools to support multiple ways of learning.
