Care Beyond the Breathing Treatment

Respiratory therapists do more than deliver therapy. We notice when a patient cannot eat without becoming dyspneic, recognize when fluid retention is worsening breathing, protect patient choices and privacy, prevent wrong-patient errors, and know when a problem needs another discipline. Work through each station using one routine: identify the problem → connect the mechanism → stay within scope → act safely → document and reassess.
