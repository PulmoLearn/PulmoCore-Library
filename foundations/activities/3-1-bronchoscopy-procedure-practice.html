Bronchoscopy: Prepare, Monitor & Respond

Flexible bronchoscopy is not just “putting a camera in the airway.” The respiratory therapist must connect the purpose of the procedure with the patient’s risk, prepare rescue equipment before sedation, monitor oxygenation and ventilation separately, assist with the correct diagnostic or therapeutic technique, recognize complications early, and continue reassessment after the scope is removed.

Patient-first rule: If the patient deteriorates, stabilize airway, breathing, oxygenation, ventilation, and hemodynamics before troubleshooting equipment or finishing the planned procedure.
