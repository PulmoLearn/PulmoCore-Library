CHECKDoes the direction, magnitude, unit, and clinical result make sense?

“Does my answer make sense?” is part of the calculation.

Example: A patient weighs 176 lb. If you calculate 387 kg, something is wrong. A kilogram is a larger unit than a pound, so the numerical value in kilograms must be smaller. You likely multiplied by 2.2 instead of dividing.

176 lb ÷ 2.2 lb/kg = 80 kg
