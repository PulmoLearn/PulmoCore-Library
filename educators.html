Built from the real pain points

RT educators should not have to build everything themselves.

PulmoLearn grew from student and faculty frustrations with fragmented course materials, expensive textbooks, inaccessible resources after a course ends, and the enormous amount of faculty time required to create meaningful clinical learning experiences from scratch.

📚

Textbook overload

Comprehensive RT texts are valuable references, but they can be overwhelming as the primary learning path. PulmoLearn organizes essential concepts into focused, interactive lessons students can revisit throughout their program.

🧩

Fragmented curriculum

Students often lose access to prior course materials just when they need them again. PulmoLearn provides one connected curriculum that supports learning across courses, clinicals, and RTE preparation.

⏱️

Faculty build burden

Slides, quizzes, competency exams, Canvas pages, breakout activities, clinical cases, alignment documents, and LMS setup take hundreds of hours to create and maintain. The educator package is designed to reduce that workload.

Why PulmoLearn exists

A curriculum designed around how RT students actually learn.

PulmoLearn was developed by an experienced respiratory therapist and RT educator to solve recurring student pain points: too much content, too many disconnected resources, limited long-term access, and not enough opportunities to apply what they know to realistic patient decisions.

The goal is not to replace every reference textbook. It is to replace the expectation that students should have to navigate thousands of pages and disconnected resources to figure out what matters most.

Students receive a structured learning path they can return to throughout school and beyond. Educators receive the teaching infrastructure to bring that curriculum into their own courses.
