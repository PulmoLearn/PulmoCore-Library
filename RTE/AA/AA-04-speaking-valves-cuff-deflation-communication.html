Speaking-valve mechanics

In through the trach, out through the upper airway

1. InspirationGas enters through the tracheostomy and one-way speaking valve.
2. Exhalation valve closesThe source states the valve does not allow exhaled gas to exit back through the tracheostomy tube.
3. Cuff must be deflatedExhaled gas needs space to move around the tracheostomy tube/cuff.
4. Upper airway must carry exhaled gasGas moves through the vocal cords and mouth, allowing voice.
Critical safety rule

The tracheostomy cuff must be deflated before a Passy-Muir speaking valve is used. Your source explicitly states that with the cuff inflated, the patient will not be able to exhale.
