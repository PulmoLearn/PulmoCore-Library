ECMO & Extracorporeal Rescue Concepts

Extracorporeal membrane oxygenation is rescue support for severe organ failure when conventional therapy is no longer enough. The RTE focus is recognizing when referral is appropriate, distinguishing venovenous from venoarterial support, and knowing that respiratory care continues after cannulation.

ECMO decision framework

VV ECMO

Your source defines venovenous ECMO as gas-exchange support for severe respiratory failure when native cardiac function is adequate.

VA ECMO

Your source defines venoarterial ECMO as combined circulatory and gas-exchange support for cardiogenic shock, cardiac arrest, or combined cardiorespiratory failure.

Referral logic

this RTE review frames ECMO as rescue support. Your ARDS source connects ECMO referral to failure of optimized conventional support.

ECMO is not “no more ventilator.”

Your source explicitly says ECMO changes conventional support goals but does not eliminate the RT role.
