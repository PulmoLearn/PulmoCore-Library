Ventilator Circuit, Setup & Pre-Use Safety

Before connecting a patient, the ventilator must be assembled correctly, connected to the required gas sources, configured with the intended humidification and filters, pass its pre-use verification, and demonstrate a safe patient-ready circuit.

Patient-ready sequence

Assemble → supply → humidify → verify → test → connect

1. Assemble the circuitYour existing equipment lesson includes inspiratory and expiratory limbs, bacteria/expiratory filters, sensors/adapters, humidification, and the patient connection as required setup content.
2. Verify gas and power sourcesThe source lesson explicitly includes oxygen supply and air supply before patient connection.
3. Select humidificationArtificial airways bypass normal upper-airway conditioning, so humidification must be intentionally selected rather than treated as an optional accessory.
4. Complete pre-use verificationThe source lesson specifically includes short self-test (SST), circuit leak testing, ventilator safety checks, and test-lung verification.
5. Check alarmsThe MVF-05 registry explicitly requires alarm checks before connecting the patient. Detailed alarm diagnosis belongs to ALM-01 through ALM-05.
6. Only then connect the patientA failed self-test, unresolved circuit leak, missing gas supply, incorrect humidification setup, or failed patient-ready check should be corrected before clinical use.
Pre-use verification is quality control.

The shared II.B.4 mapping is not “paperwork.” The purpose is to catch setup, circuit, supply, leak, and function problems before they become a patient event.

Heated humidifier: the source identifies active heated humidification as the preferred approach when thick secretions or prolonged mechanical ventilation require greater humidity.

Which problems must be fixed before patient connection?

Ventilator setup:
  • Heated humidifier connected
  • HME still attached at the patient Y
  • Dependent circuit contains pooled condensate near the patient
  • SST reports a circuit leak
  • Alarm verification has not been completed
