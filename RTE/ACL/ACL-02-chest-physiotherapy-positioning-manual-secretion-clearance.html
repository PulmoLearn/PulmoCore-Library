Chest Physiotherapy, Positioning & Manual Secretion Clearance

Use anatomy and gravity to position the targeted region, add percussion or vibration when appropriate, then move the mobilized secretions out with cough, huff, or suction when needed.

Primary: III.B.1 Postural drainage, percussion, vibrationShared: III.A.1 Proper positioning
LocalizeUse imaging, breath sounds, and the clinical picture to identify the congested region.
PositionChoose the postural-drainage posture that uses gravity for the target.
MobilizePercussion/vibration help move retained secretions.
ClearSit up, deep breathe, cough/huff; suction if the patient cannot clear.

CPT sequence

Assess → target → position → mobilize → cough → reassess

Assess firstThe lab competency reviews history, chest radiographs, atelectasis/infiltrates/mucus plugs, contraindications, respirations, SpO₂ and lung sounds before choosing the region to drain.
Target the anatomyThe same competency says to begin with apical segments and progress downward. A LindseyJones RTE item specifically places lateral/posterior basal disease in Trendelenburg with the head down 30°.
Position long enough to workThe lab competency keeps the patient in the selected posture for 3–15 minutes depending on condition, tolerance and secretions.
Percuss/vibrate the targetSource technique uses cupped hands or a mechanical percussor over the selected region while avoiding bony structures; percussion may be used alone or with vibration.
Clear after mobilizationAfter positioning, the source has the patient sit up, take deep breaths and cough; suction equipment is prepared if the patient cannot clear secretions.
EvaluateReassess response in each position and modify as needed; record tolerance, vital signs, lung sounds, cough effectiveness and sputum.
Primary task versus shared positioning task

For III.B.1, choose and perform the clearance posture correctly. For shared III.A.1, positioning must still maintain airway patency, artificial-airway security, oxygen delivery, monitoring lines and patient safety while the drainage position is used.
