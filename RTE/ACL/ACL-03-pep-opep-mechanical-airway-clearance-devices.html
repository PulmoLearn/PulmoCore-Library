PEP, OPEP & Mechanical Airway-Clearance Devices

Compare what each device actually does: PEP splints airways during exhalation, OPEP adds oscillation, HFCWO mobilizes secretions from the chest wall, and MI-E simulates a cough when respiratory muscles are too weak.

Which device matches the mechanism?

Device comparison

Mechanism first, device name second

PEPThe pediatric source describes exhalation against resistance, helping splint airways and move gas behind secretions.
OPEPThe source defines oscillatory PEP as expiratory resistance plus vibration. Examples include Acapella and Flutter.
HFCWOAn external inflatable garment creates rapid chest-wall oscillation to mobilize secretions; the source warns it does not replace an effective cough.
MI-EMechanical insufflation-exsufflation applies positive pressure followed by negative pressure to simulate cough in respiratory muscle weakness.
Primary decision rule

Ask whether the problem is airway splinting/mucus movement, mucus oscillation, external chest-wall mobilization, or weak cough mechanics. Then choose the device that fixes that mechanism.
