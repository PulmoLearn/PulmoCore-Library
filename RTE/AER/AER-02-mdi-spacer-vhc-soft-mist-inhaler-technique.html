Which device strategy fits the patient?

Start with coordination, inspiratory effort, and ability to follow technique.

Standard MDI technique

Slow inhalation + correct timing + breath hold

The source competency guide gives the sequence below.

Verify the medication and dose.
Shake the inhaler when required by the product.
Exhale before inhalation.
Seal the lips around the mouthpiece.
Begin a slow, deep inhalation while actuating the canister.
Continue inhaling fully.
Hold the breath as tolerated, up to about 10 seconds in the lesson examples.
Wait the required interval before another puff when applicable.
RTE trap

Rapid MDI inhalation increases turbulence and proximal impaction. A patient who says the medication “hits the tongue” or tastes strongly in the mouth may have poor coordination, inhaling too fast, or need a spacer/VHC.
