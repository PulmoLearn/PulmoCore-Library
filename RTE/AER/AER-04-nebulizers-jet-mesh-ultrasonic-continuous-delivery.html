Same goal, different aerosol engines

The source lesson contrasts how each device generates aerosol and where efficiency is lost.

DeviceHow it worksSource strengths / limitations
Jet SVNCompressed air or oxygen drives the nebulizer.Common, inexpensive, useful in acute distress; requires driving gas, has residual volume, standard continuous jet wastes medication during exhalation.
Breath-enhanced jetOne-way valves increase aerosol delivery during inspiration.Reduces expiratory waste compared with standard continuous jet.
Breath-actuated jetAerosol is generated only during inhalation.Highly efficient, but requires enough triggering flow.
UltrasonicPiezoelectric transducer creates aerosol; no compressed gas required.High output and quiet; transducer heat can be a problem for heat-sensitive medications.
Vibrating mesh (VMN)Liquid passes through thousands of tiny holes in a vibrating mesh.Minimal residual volume, quiet, no added gas flow, controlled particle size, no medication heating.
Big idea

Visible mist does not prove equivalent lung dose. Driving flow, residual volume, cup position, device design, mask seal, breathing pattern, and respiratory-support setup all change delivery.
