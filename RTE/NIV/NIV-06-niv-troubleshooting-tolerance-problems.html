NIV Troubleshooting & Tolerance Problems

When NIV is not working, identify the actual problem before changing pressure: leak, discomfort, dryness, aerophagia, asynchrony, poor triggering, rebreathing, inadequate ventilation, or overall intolerance.

Primary: II.A.4 CPAP/NPPV with patient interfacesNo shared mapping
Socratic troubleshooting string

What is the actual problem?

Framework

Identify → correct → reassess

Leak: distinguish expected exhalation-port flow from unwanted mask-edge or connection leak.
Trigger/synchrony: visible effort that does not consistently produce supported breaths suggests poor interaction; large leaks can worsen it.
Rebreathing: verify the intentional exhalation pathway is open. Your supplied single-limb source also flags too-low EPAP as a rebreathing concern.
Inadequate ventilation: first exclude leak/setup problems; then use NIV-04 pressure-support logic.
Troubleshoot the cause, not the display.
A leak value, low VT, or missed trigger is a clue—not the diagnosis.
Source boundary: this RTE review explicitly requires dryness, aerophagia, mask discomfort, and patient intolerance. The retrieved NIV sources do not give detailed treatment algorithms for those symptoms, so this lesson teaches recognition and safe setup reassessment without inventing device-specific prescriptions.
