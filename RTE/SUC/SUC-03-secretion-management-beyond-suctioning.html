What part of secretion clearance is failing?

Mechanism-first framework

Condition → mobilize → cough → remove what remains

Mucus hydration/conditioningYour airway-defense source teaches that dehydration increases mucus viscosity, slows mucociliary transport, and promotes retention.
Mucociliary transportFunctional cilia plus appropriately hydrated mucus move trapped material toward the pharynx.
Cough mechanicsA patient can have normal mucus hydration but still retain secretions because expiratory muscle weakness prevents effective cough clearance.
Mechanical cough augmentationYour pediatric source uses mechanical insufflation-exsufflation (MI-E) when respiratory muscle weakness causes recurrent mucus plugging.
Key distinction

Suction removes reachable secretions. It does not restore mucus hydration, ciliary transport, or cough strength. If secretions keep returning because the patient cannot generate an effective cough, the care plan must address that mechanism.
