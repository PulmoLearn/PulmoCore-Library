Recognizing When Suction Is Needed

Suction because the patient shows evidence of retained material that needs removal—not simply because a clock, routine, or artificial airway is present.

Core exam rule

Presence of an ETT or tracheostomy does not automatically equal “suction now.” The registry specifically requires students to distinguish indication-based suctioning from unnecessary routine suction.
