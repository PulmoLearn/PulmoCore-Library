High-yield distinction

Bronchodilator = opens constricted airway. Controller/anti-inflammatory therapy = reduces the inflammatory process driving asthma over time or during a significant exacerbation.

Source boundary: This RTE review magnesium in severe asthma, but the supplied course files retrieved for this lesson do not provide a dose or detailed mechanism. This lesson therefore tests its role only as a severe-asthma adjunct concept and does not invent dosing.
