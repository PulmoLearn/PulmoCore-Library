Core framework

Severity first. Phenotype second. Targeted therapy third.

1. Confirm advanced-disease contextthis RTE review places biologics in severe asthma and phenotype-directed therapy, not routine first-line rescue treatment.
2. Identify the inflammatory patternYour source set supports eosinophils as an asthma-associated inflammatory clue and frames biologic choice around inflammatory phenotype.
3. Match targeted therapyThe NBRC outline specifically names dupilumab (Dupixent) as an example of an asthma biologic.
4. Reassess the care planPersistent symptoms, frequent exacerbations, rescue use, technique, and adherence all matter before or after recommending escalation.
