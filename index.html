Complete RT Curriculum

150+ interactive lessons across seven major curriculum areas.

PulmoLearn is designed to support learners throughout respiratory therapy school while building the clinical reasoning needed for the 2027 NBRC Respiratory Therapy Examination.

🩺

Pulmonary Assessment

Patient interviews, physical assessment, breath sounds, documentation, diagnostic testing, imaging, ABG sampling, infection control, oxygenation assessment, and integrated patient assessment.

🧭

Foundations of Respiratory Care

Communication, medical math, monitoring, laboratory interpretation, pulmonary function testing, ethics, documentation, and foundational respiratory care skills.

🧬

Respiratory Physiology

Respiratory anatomy, mechanics of ventilation, gas exchange, lung volumes, acid-base physiology, V/Q relationships, oxygen transport, hemodynamics, and applied physiology.

🫁

Mechanical Ventilation

Ventilator setup and initiation, modes, settings, ABG-guided adjustments, patient-ventilator interaction, troubleshooting, ventilator graphics, monitoring, weaning, and clinical decision-making.

👶

Neonatal & Pediatric Respiratory Care

Neonatal and pediatric assessment, stabilization, oxygenation, respiratory support, ventilation, congenital and acquired disorders, monitoring, reassessment, and escalation of care.

🫁

Respiratory Diseases

Interactive disease lessons connecting pathophysiology, recognition, differentiation, diagnostics, respiratory management, reassessment, complications, and escalation of care.

🔧

Respiratory Equipment & Procedures

Oxygen delivery, aerosol therapy, humidity, medical gases, airway management, respiratory medications, blood gas monitoring, cardiovascular monitoring, respiratory equipment, and clinical procedures.

🎓

RTE Preparation Throughout

Board preparation is integrated throughout the curriculum so students continuously connect course concepts with 2027 RTE-style recognition, interpretation, clinical judgment, treatment, reassessment, and escalation.
