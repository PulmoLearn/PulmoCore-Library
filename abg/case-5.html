Your interpretation routine

Five Decisions, Every Time

1. pH: <7.35 acidosis; 7.35–7.45 normal range; >7.45 alkalosis.
2. Primary: PaCO₂ changes opposite pH; HCO₃⁻ changes with pH.
3. Compensation: Count abnormal PaCO₂/HCO₃⁻ values, then check whether the other system moves in the expected direction. Normal-range pH does not rule out a disorder.
4. Oxygenation: PaO₂ 80–100 normal, 60–79 mild, 40–59 moderate, <40 severe hypoxemia.
5. Interpretation: Combine compensation + primary disorder + oxygenation.
Clinical note: This is an introductory pattern-recognition method. In clinical practice, use expected compensation formulas to check for mixed disorders; pH +2 alone cannot prove compensation.
