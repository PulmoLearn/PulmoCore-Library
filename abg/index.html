How Each Case Works

Each case is built like a mini PulmoLearn lesson. You will unlock one step at a time and receive immediate feedback before moving forward.

Step 1: Decide whether the pH is acidotic, alkalotic, or normal.
Step 2: Identify the primary respiratory or metabolic pattern.
Step 3: Determine whether compensation is present.
Step 4: Assess oxygenation using the PaO₂.
Step 5: Build the final complete ABG interpretation.
