Interactive Practice

Follow the 5 Guided Learning Steps

Begin with the interpretation guide, then complete the five interactive learning Steps in order. Each Step builds on the last with guided ABGs and feedback.

Start Here

How to Interpret an ABG

Review the full ABG interpretation method, then begin Step 1 to practice identifying acidemia, alkalemia, and normal pH.

 Step 1

Start With the pH

Identify acidemia, alkalemia, or a normal-range pH first. Practice reading pH before deciding what is causing the imbalance.

 Step 2

Respiratory or Metabolic?

Work through all four primary acid-base disorders. Compare pH with PaCO₂ and HCO₃⁻ to identify respiratory versus metabolic causes.

 Step 3

Is Compensation Happening?

Use the pH +1 / pH +2 framework across three ABGs to recognize no compensation, partial compensation, and when to question a mixed disorder.

 Step 4

When the pH Looks Normal

Compare a truly normal ABG with compensated patterns. Use the 7.40 midpoint as a clue when pH is normal but PaCO₂ and HCO₃⁻ are abnormal.

 Step 5

Put It All Together

Interpret three complete ABGs with progressively less guidance: pH, primary disorder, compensation, oxygenation, and final interpretation.
