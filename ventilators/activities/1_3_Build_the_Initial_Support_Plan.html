Station 1 · Guided calculation

Calculate PBW, Estimate IBW, and Size the Breath

Use the exact PBW formula for PBW-based VT and the quick IBW method only when the question asks for an estimate.

0 / 6 mastered
Exact PBW: male 50 + 2.3(inches over 60) · female 45.5 + 2.3(inches over 60)
NBRC quick IBW estimate: 50 + 2(inches − 60), then +2 male or −2 female

Keep the labels straight: PBW is used as a lung-size proxy for VT selection. IBW estimates ideal body mass. The values may be close, but the shortcut does not replace the exact PBW formula.
