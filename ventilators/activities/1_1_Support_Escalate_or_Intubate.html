Decide Whether Support Is Working

Interpret the direction of change and correct the actual failure mechanism.

Locked

NIV response

After 40 minutes of bilevel NIV, RR decreases 34→25/min, pH rises 7.23→7.29, PaCO₂ falls 76→66 mm Hg, effort improves, and the patient remains alert.

NIV failure

During NIV, pH falls 7.22→7.15, PaCO₂ rises, distress worsens, and the patient becomes unable to manage secretions.

Ineffective bag-mask ventilation

No chest rise is seen, snoring is heard, and little leak is felt around the mask.

Persistent mask leak

After airway positioning, a single rescuer still cannot maintain a seal and air escapes with each breath.
