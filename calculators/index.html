Oxygenation

P/F Ratio Calculator

Calculate PaO₂/FiO₂ and interpret oxygenation impairment severity.

 Gradient

A-a Gradient Calculator

CaO₂ Calculator

Calculate arterial oxygen content using hemoglobin, SaO₂, and PaO₂.

DO₂ Calculator

Calculate oxygen delivery from cardiac output and arterial oxygen content.

Mean Airway Pressure Calculator

Estimate MAP from pressure and timing inputs and connect it to oxygenation.

Evaluate Ventilation, CO₂ Clearance, and Dead Space

Ventilation

Minute Ventilation Calculator

Calculate VE from respiratory rate and tidal volume.

 CO₂ Clearance

Alveolar Ventilation Calculator

Calculate effective alveolar ventilation using RR, VT, and dead space.

Dead Space VD/VT Calculator

Compliance

 Compliance

Dynamic Compliance Calculator

 Resistance

IBW / Tidal Volume Calculator

Estimate predicted body weight and 4, 6, and 8 mL/kg VT targets.
