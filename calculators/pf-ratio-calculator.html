Formula

P/F Ratio Formula

P/F Ratio = PaO₂ ÷ FiO₂

Example: PaO₂ 80 on FiO₂ 0.40 → 80 ÷ 0.40 = 200
P/F RatioInterpretation
> 300Better oxygenation
201–300Mild oxygenation impairment range
101–200Moderate oxygenation impairment range
≤ 100Severe oxygenation impairment range
