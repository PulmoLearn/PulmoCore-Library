2027 RTE Alignment

Built around the new NBRC Respiratory Therapy Examination

PulmoLearn curriculum is designed to support the knowledge and depth of clinical judgment emphasized on the 2027 NBRC Respiratory Therapy Examination (RTE). Rather than separating board preparation from course learning, RTE-relevant reasoning is integrated throughout the curriculum so students practice connecting patient findings with appropriate respiratory care decisions.

Currently Published Courses

Learn across the respiratory therapy curriculum.

These courses are currently available to PulmoLearn members. New lessons, activities, and additional curriculum are being developed and are added to lifetime memberships as they are released.

🧭

Foundations of Respiratory Care

Published

Build the core skills used throughout respiratory therapy practice, including professional communication, medical math, oxygenation calculations, pulmonary function testing, monitoring, laboratory interpretation, ABGs, bronchoscopy, ECG interpretation, acid-base disorders, ethics, nutrition, and documentation.

Clinical Focus Foundational RT concepts, quantitative reasoning, diagnostics, professional practice, and integrated respiratory decision-making.
🧬

Respiratory Physiology

Published

Explore airway defense, respiratory anatomy, mechanics of ventilation, lung volumes, acid-base physiology, ventilatory control, V/Q relationships, oxygenation, sleep-disordered breathing, cardiovascular physiology, hemodynamics, fluid balance, and newborn transition.

Clinical Focus Understanding why respiratory abnormalities occur and connecting physiologic changes to patient assessment and treatment decisions.
🩺

Pulmonary Assessment

Published

Develop systematic respiratory patient assessment skills through interviewing, physical examination, pulmonary function testing, blood gas analysis, oxygenation assessment, ECG monitoring, diagnostic imaging, infection control, clinical documentation, and integrated pulmonary assessment.

Clinical Focus Recognizing important patient findings, interpreting diagnostic data, documenting accurately, and building an appropriate respiratory care plan.
🫁

Ventilator Management

Published

Learn to initiate mechanical ventilation, choose initial settings, make ABG-guided adjustments, evaluate patient-ventilator interaction, troubleshoot alarms and deterioration, and interpret ventilator waveforms and loops in clinical context.

Clinical Focus Safe ventilator initiation, targeted adjustment, patient-first troubleshooting, graphics interpretation, and bedside decision-making.
💊

Respiratory Pharmacology

Published

Study medication principles, dosage calculations, respiratory receptors, aerosol drug delivery, bronchodilators, anti-inflammatory medications, secretion-management therapies, surfactant, anti-infectives, cardiovascular and critical-care medications, therapeutic gases, disease-specific pharmacotherapy, and emergency medications.

Clinical Focus Medication selection, administration, adverse effects, monitoring, therapeutic response, and respiratory therapist responsibilities.
🔧

Equipment & Procedures

Published

Learn respiratory equipment setup, operation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and clinical use across oxygen delivery, aerosol therapy, humidification, airway management, NIV, ventilator setup, artificial airway care, suctioning, bronchoscopy assistance, specialty gases, transport, pleural drainage, invasive monitoring, extubation, and other bedside procedures.

Clinical Focus Equipment setup, safety checks, patient monitoring, troubleshooting, procedure assistance, and readiness for ICU practice.
👶

Neonatal & Pediatric Respiratory Care

Published

Follow neonatal and pediatric respiratory care from fetal lung development and newborn transition through resuscitation, neonatal disease, noninvasive and invasive ventilation, pediatric assessment, airway management, ARDS, obstructive and infectious disorders, critical illness, ECMO concepts, transport, home care, family education, and end-of-life support.

Clinical Focus Age-specific assessment, stabilization, respiratory support, ventilation, disease management, critical care, and family-centered care.
🫁

Respiratory Diseases

Published

Apply respiratory care concepts across obstructive, restrictive, infectious, vascular, pleural, neonatal, pediatric, neuromuscular, traumatic, environmental, cardiopulmonary, and sleep-related disorders. Disease lessons connect pathophysiology with recognition, diagnostics, respiratory management, reassessment, and escalation.

Clinical Focus Disease recognition, differentiation, treatment priorities, respiratory support, reassessment, complications, and escalation.
